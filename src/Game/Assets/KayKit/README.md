# Historical KayKit asset notices

The KayKit 3D models, buffers, atlases and import mappings are no longer bundled.
This directory retains their CC0 license texts for attribution history, not a
runtime or prototype fallback. See [current 3D provenance and mapping](../../../../docs/assets.md)
for the active palette. The [historical manifest](../../../../docs/asset-history/kaykit-manifest.json)
retains official source paths, pinned Git commits or original archive members,
retrieval dates, SHA-256 checksums and extraction relationships for the removed files.
No paid tiers, third-party mirrors or runtime downloads were used.

The former selected free packs were:

- [Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0): buildings, terrain and village props; [CC0 notice](Medieval/LICENSE.txt).
- [Prototype Bits 1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Prototype-Bits-1.0): Dummy Base, Can A and Cube Prototype Small; [CC0 notice](Prototype/LICENSE.txt).
- [Adventurers Character Pack 1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0/tree/672074b73ba276876a19e8816ecdc5241817ab47): Knight, Rogue, Barbarian and Mage rigs and weapons; [CC0 notice](Characters/LICENSE.txt).
- Skeleton character pack, pinned at official commit `15b62b9bad122f72926c10fb14d622c73819fa54`: corresponding enemy rigs and weapons; [CC0 notice](Characters/Skeletons_LICENSE.txt). Official per-file sources remain in the historical manifest.
- [Resource Bits 1.0 Free](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/resource-bits): Gold Bars and free wood logs/planks/pallets from the official Free download (upload 13266824); [CC0 notice](Resource/LICENSE.txt). The archive checksum remains in the historical manifest; paid coin/food extras were not included.

Historical rendered-pose and integration results remain in [verification evidence](../../../../docs/verification.md);
they do not describe the current rig, weapon bindings or environment composition.
